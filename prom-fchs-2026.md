## Color Palette

### Primary Colors — MA3 Reference Values

| Name | Use | Approx RGB | MA3 Color Picker Notes |
|------|-----|------------|------------------------|
| **Patriot Blue** | Main atmospheric wash, backdrop | R:80 G:160 B:255 | Sky/powder blue — cool, clean |
| **Patriot Gold** | Accents, key light, reveals | R:255 G:185 B:20 | Warm amber-gold, not orange |
| **White** | Key light on performers/court, clean looks | R:255 G:255 B:255 | Full CTB correction on tungsten |
| **Deep Blue** | Low-energy dance floor, slow dance base | R:20 G:60 B:180 | Richer, more saturated blue |
| **Soft Gold** | Slow dance wash, romantic moments | R:255 G:210 B:100 | Warmer, pastel — flattering on skin |
| **Champagne** | Photo-op / arrival ambient | R:255 G:235 B:180 | Near-white warm, universally flattering |
| **Lavender** | Transition / crossfade filler | R:180 G:140 B:255 | Bridges blue and gold gracefully |

### Palette Rules
- **Blue** is the atmosphere color — it fills the room and the air
- **Gold** is the energy color — it cuts through blue on peaks and reveals
- Slow dances: Soft Gold wash on performers, Deep Blue ambient fill
- Strobe/flash moments: White or Gold only — never blue strobe (looks cold/clinical)
- Proscenium strips: run in Gold during high energy, Blue during ambient

## Show Flow & Cue Map

### Pre-Show / Arrival (30–60 min before)
**Goal:** Photo-worthy, flattering, excited energy without giving away the full show

- **Wash:** Champagne/soft white wash, 60% intensity
- **Spots (FOH):** Slow pan sweep, patriot blue, 25% intensity
- **Proscenium:** Patriot blue, low dimmer (30%), gentle bottom→top pulse (0.1 Hz, 12%)
- **FX:** Breathing Room recipe (#7) — very slow, 0.15 Hz, all together
- **NO strobes, NO hard movement** — this is photo time

---

### Grand Entrance (couples walk in)
**Goal:** Big moment, each couple gets their own spotlight feel

**Executor layout:**
- **Exec A:** Entrance walk look (triggered once per couple or looped)
- **Exec B:** Applause flash (manual button)

**Entrance Walk Look:**
- FOH spots: tight zoom, white key light, follow or zone-based on entry path
- Mid/back wash: Patriot Blue full, 80%
- Proscenium: Gold, 100%, steady (no FX — frame is the statement)
- Side wash: Soft Gold at 40% — warms the faces

**Applause Flash (manual trigger, Exec B):**
- All wash: bump to Patriot Gold 100%, 0 fade
- Follow cue: fade back to entrance look, 1.5s

---

### Opening Dance Floor — Warm Up
**Goal:** Energy is building, crowd is finding the floor, DJ is setting the tone

- **Wash:** Patriot Blue dominant (70%), Gold accents (40%) on back/upstage wash
- **Spots:** Aerial Beam Storm recipe (#2) — Pan/Tilt orbital, medium speed
- **Proscenium:** Gold, Dimmer Sine 0.5 Hz, 35% size, bottom→top fan
- **Blinders:** Armed, not firing yet — save them
- **Rate:** 50% (1x default)
- **FX stacked:** Hue Chase FOH→Stage (#3) at low Rate — color wave visible but subtle

---

### Peak Dance Floor — High Energy
**Goal:** Full send. The floor is packed, DJ is on a run, everyone is in it.

- **Wash:** Alternate Blue/Gold per 8-bar phrase via executor toggle
- **Spots:** Aerial Beam Storm at 75% Rate, add Pan Pendulum Cross (#4) on separate layer
- **Blinders:** Strobe Depth Chase (#5) — mid tempo, stage outward
- **Proscenium:** Gold, VU Meter recipe (#19) — bottom→top chase at 1–2 Hz
- **FX:** Room Pulse Depth Wave (#1) — Stage→FOH direction, 2 Hz, 70%
- **Rate Master:** Push to 75–100% for peak moments

**Big Drop moment:**
- Bass Drop Punch (#6) on dedicated button
- Blinder full strobe (Square, 8 Hz) — hold for 4–8 beats, cut clean

---

### Prom Court Processional
**Goal:** Each member of the court gets a clean, distinguished look walking in. Prestigious.

- **House wash to blackout** or **dim blue ambient** as court announcement begins
- **Spots (FOH):** Tight white key, one or two fixtures per court member position
- **Back wash (upstage):** Patriot Gold, 60% — warm backlight on each person
- **Proscenium:** Gold, 100%, steady — no FX, this is framing not flash
- **Blinders:** OFF — no audience blinding during this moment
- **Movement:** None. Static looks only. Court processional is not a rave.

**Name callout flash (per person, manual trigger):**
- Proscenium strips: bump to full white, 0 fade → fade back to gold, 1s
- Mid wash: flash to Patriot Gold 100%, return in 0.8s

---

### Crowning Moment — King & Queen Reveal
**Goal:** The biggest singular moment of the night. Build → reveal → explode.

**Build sequence (Exec — 3 cues in follow):**

| Cue | What Happens | Timing |
|-----|-------------|--------|
| 1 | All wash deep blue, 40% — room gets mysterious | Manual trigger |
| 2 | Spots tighten to center stage, white key — 2 fixtures only | 1s fade |
| 3 | Proscenium dims to 10% gold — everything else fades | 3s fade |

**Reveal (single button press):**
- **All fixtures → Patriot Gold, 100%**, Zoom wide, 0 fade
- **Proscenium strips → full white burst**, 0 fade
- **Blinders/strobes → 3 short flashes** (8 Hz, 3 × 0.5s, gaps between), then out
- **Confetti/sparkle FX** if available on any fixture: trigger here
- **Wash settles:** Gold on back/upstage, Blue ambient fill, 3s fade into hold

**Hold look (post-crown):**
- Patriot Blue + Gold together, warm, flattering
- Proscenium: soft gold, slow pulse (0.2 Hz, 15% size)
- Spots: gentle tilt wave on court positions
- This is photo time — keep it beautiful and still

---

### Slow Dance / Ballad
**Goal:** Romantic, warm, flattering. People look good. The room feels intimate.

- **Wash:** Soft Gold on performers (60%), Deep Blue ambient fill (30%)
- **Spots (FOH):** Wide zoom, Soft Gold, 50% — warm key wash not spot
- **Back wash:** Lavender at 25% — subtle, fills shadows with something beautiful
- **Proscenium:** Gold, very slow pulse (0.1 Hz, 10%), soft
- **Blinders:** 0% — completely off
- **FX:**
  - Breathing Room (#7) — 0.2 Hz, 20% size, all together
  - Key Light Heartbeat (#12) — 0.15 Hz, 10% size on FOH wash only

**Avoid:** Blue on performers during slow dance — it reads cold on camera and on skin.
Gold and lavender are universally flattering under any skin tone.

---

### Dance Floor Return (post-slow dance energy rebuild)
**Goal:** Smooth ramp back up — don't jar the room, let the DJ lead.

- Start in slow dance look
- Push Rate Master from 25% → 50% over 2–3 songs
- Gradually reintroduce Blue wash (crossfade Gold → Blue dominant)
- Bring blinders back in (not firing yet)
- By song 3: full peak dance floor look restored

---

### Last Dance / Finale
**Goal:** Memorable ending. Everyone is emotionally invested. Make it feel significant.

- Bring energy DOWN first — back to soft look (echoes the slow dance palette)
- Final song announcement: Proscenium → Gold 100%, all wash → soft mix
- As song builds: Proscenium to Rig Cascade (#17) on the final chorus
- **Song out:** hold last frame for 3–5 seconds, then slow fade to blackout (10s)
- **Post-song:** house lights up to 30% — don't snap them, let it breathe

## Programming Notes

**Before patch arrives:**
- Build color presets first — Patriot Blue, Gold, Soft Gold, Deep Blue, Champagne, Lavender
- These presets underpin every look and can be stored before any fixture is addressed

**Once patch is in:**
1. Address and profile all fixtures
2. Build groups: FOH Spots, Mid Spots, Stage Spots, FOH Wash, Mid Wash, Stage Wash,
   Proscenium Left, Proscenium Right, All Blinders, All Strobes
3. Lay out fixture positions in the Layout View — depth axis is Y, width is X
4. Build Presets: color presets above, then position presets (aerial, down, crowd)
5. Stack cues onto executors per page layout above
6. Apply FX recipes from fx-recipe-library.md as executor layers

**Rate Master:** Assign to Fader 2, Page 1. Set range 0.25x–2x.
**Size Master:** Assign to Fader 3, Page 1. Controls FX amplitude live.
